# Moodle Site Backup

To use this site in its current state, follow these steps:

1. **Database:** Import `moodle_backup.sql` into your local `mysql` or `moodle_test` database.
2. **Moodle Data (Images & H5P):** * Download the `moodledata_backup.zip` from [INSERT YOUR GOOGLE DRIVE/CLOUD LINK HERE].
   * Unzip it and place the `moodledata` folder directly into your `C:\xampp\` directory.
3. **Run the Site:** Update your local `config.php` to match your local paths.
